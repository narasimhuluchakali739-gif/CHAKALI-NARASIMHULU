## 🧠 About Me

> **"I don't just use AI — I engineer it."**

I'm **Chakali Narasimhulu**, a passionate **AI Engineer**, builder, and content creator. I love building intelligent systems, learning new technologies, and sharing my journey with others.

I believe the best way to grow is to **learn, build, share, and improve every day**. I am currently exploring AI, machine learning, full-stack development, and real-world AI projects.

### 🚀 What I Do

- 🤖 Build AI and machine learning projects
- 💻 Create web apps and intelligent systems
- 📹 Share AI tutorials, project walkthroughs, and tech content
- 🌱 Learn in public and improve every day
- 🤝 Collaborate with builders, learners, and creators
